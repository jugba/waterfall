# Spend the first marketing money on capsule art, before trailer, ads or PR

- Area: marketing
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, desk research. Zukowski 2022-03-08 (https://howtomarketagame.com/2022/03/08/what-should-you-spend-money-on-if-you-have-a-small-marketing-budget/); Zukowski 2020-04-13 "one new image increased sales by 20x" (https://howtomarketagame.com/2020/04/13/how-one-new-image-increased-sales-by-20x/)
- Confidence: medium-high (strong practitioner consensus; the evidence is case studies)
- Apply when: allocating each game's art and marketing budget, and scheduling asset work.

The capsule is the click-through gate for every Steam surface. After 2026-06, those surfaces include wider images and the Personal Calendar.

A montage trailer is acceptable; an amateur capsule is not. The capsule must:
- show the genre at thumbnail size;
- have a legible logo;
- have one focal subject;
- match the screenshots.

Recheck the Steamworks asset specs each cycle, because the 2026 redesign changed image formats.
