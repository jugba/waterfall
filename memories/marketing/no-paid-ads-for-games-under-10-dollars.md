# Do not run paid ads for games at or under $10; at about $1 per wishlist they break even at best

- Area: marketing
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, desk research. Ad benchmark of about $1/wishlist ($1–2 acceptable) via secondary summaries of Zukowski, unverified (https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/indie-game-marketing-budget); Danchi Days Reddit ads case, about $1.50/wishlist (https://buttondown.com/melodicambient/archive/danchi-days-3600-wishlists-through-reddit-ads-was); conversion figures from marketing/wishlist-conversion-0-1-to-0-15x-first-week
- Confidence: medium
- Apply when: anyone proposes ad spend for a game priced at $10 or less.

The arithmetic:
- Month 1 brings 0.22 sales per wishlist.
- Net per sale on a $9.99 game is about $5–6 (rule of thumb, unverified).
- So $1 of ads returns about $1.2 in month 1, before the cost of making creatives.
- Over a year it rises to about 0.7 sales per wishlist, but mostly at discounted prices.

Exception: a capped test of $10–20/day for 1–2 weeks to measure cost per wishlist for a genre we will reuse. Reddit and Meta ads beat Google Search, and Facebook boosted posts do not work.
