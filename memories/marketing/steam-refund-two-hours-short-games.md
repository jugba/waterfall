# Steam refunds anything under 2 hours played within 14 days — short games need >2h of value or a low price

- Area: marketing
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, https://store.steampowered.com/steam_refunds ; https://help.steampowered.com/faqs/view/5FDE-BA65-ACCE-A411 ; EA playtime counts since 2024: https://gamespot.com/articles/steam-refund-policy-changes-have-closed-an-early-access-loophole/1100-6522898/
- Confidence: high (policy); medium (impact on our games, untested)
- Apply when: scoping game length, replayability and price for a one-month game.

Players get a refund for any reason within 14 days of purchase if playtime is under 2 hours (Early
Access/Advanced Access playtime counts). A one-month game that can be finished in under 2 hours can
be completed and refunded. Scope for ≥ 2–3 hours of first-play value (replay loops, unlocks, endless
mode) or price low enough that refunding isn't worth it, and record refund % in every postmortem.
