# Put the Steam Coming Soon page live ~5 weeks before launch (official minimum is 2 weeks)

- Area: marketing
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/coming_soon ; https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/wishlist
- Confidence: medium (2-week minimum is official; the 5-week target is our planning choice)
- Apply when: planning store page timing for any game.

Valve requires a public Coming Soon page for ≥ 2 weeks before release (also before Early Access is
playable). Everyone who has the game wishlisted at release gets an email/push notification, so the
Coming Soon window is our wishlist-collection window. With review lead times of up to 12 business
days and a 7-business-day store page review, going live at T-5 weeks gives both wishlists and slack.
On a monthly cadence this means game N+1's page goes live while game N is still in development.
