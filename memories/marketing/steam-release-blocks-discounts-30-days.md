# Releasing on Steam blocks all discounts except the launch discount for 30 days — release ≥30 days before a seasonal sale

- Area: marketing
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/discounts ; https://www.gamesradar.com/valve-lets-steam-launch-discounts-double-in-length-so-the-savings-now-last-between-7-and-14-days
- Confidence: high
- Apply when: choosing a release date near a seasonal sale, setting launch pricing.

Launch discount: max 40%, lasts 7–14 days, must be configured before release (Valve suggests 10–15%).
After that: no discount for 30 days after release (EA and full release each count), 30 days after a
price increase, or within 30 days of another discount (seasonal sales are exempt from that last one
only). Discount cutoffs: Winter 2026 sale (Dec 17) → release by Nov 17; Spring 2027 (Mar 18) → by
Feb 16; Summer 2027 (Jun 24) → by May 25.
