# Since Steam's 2026-06 redesign, Popular Upcoming needs about 80k wishlists, so we rely on tags and the Personal Calendar instead

- Area: marketing
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, desk research. GamesRadar 2026 (https://www.gamesradar.com/games/valve-made-it-roughly-15-times-harder-for-indie-games-to-reach-a-coveted-steam-ranking-but-one-expert-says-an-understated-new-steam-feature-is-doing-gods-work/); PC Guide 2026 (https://www.pcguide.com/news/steams-new-100000-wishlist-rule-means-many-indie-devs-will-have-to-rely-on-a-different-feature-to-be-discovered/); Creative Bloq 2026-06 (https://www.creativebloq.com/3d/video-game-design/is-steams-more-modern-redesign-good-or-bad-for-indie-game-developers)
- Confidence: low-medium (thresholds are inferred by developers; Valve publishes none)
- Apply when: setting pre-launch wishlist goals, or deciding where pre-launch effort goes.

Before the 2026-06-04 store refresh, the old rule of thumb was 5–10k wishlists to reach Popular Upcoming. After it, reports say about 80k, and some say 100k. A 4–6 week Coming Soon window will not get us there.

The new tag-personalised Personal Calendar reportedly favours smaller games. One developer claimed 300–3,000 wishlists/day from it, which is unverified.

New & Trending depends on a secret revenue threshold that varies by country and language. Wishlists matter there only indirectly.

So: invest in accurate tags (top 5 and top 15 ordered by relevance), capsule clarity and localisation, not in chasing Popular Upcoming.
