# Next Fest registration requires the base game to be public as Coming Soon at registration, so the store page must be submitted about 3 weeks before the deadline

- Area: marketing
- Date / source: 2026-11-27, projects/_research/game-003-direction.md §5. The Steamworks Feb 2027 Next Fest page (https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest/feb_2027) was read via web-search extract, because the host is blocked here. Third-party dates come from steampageanalyzer.com and gamineai.com.
- Confidence: medium-high (official wording seen only in extracts; the Press Preview date is third-party)
- Apply when: planning any game that will enter Next Fest, and setting onboarding or fee deadlines.

Eligibility is "visible on Steam as Coming Soon at the time of registration". It is not enough to make the page public by the fest. Feb 2027 dates:
- registration closes Jan 10;
- all items must be submitted for review by Feb 8;
- the Press Preview starts about Feb 11 (third-party);
- Valve advises having the demo approved about 6 weeks before the fest.

The demo app also needs its own store-presence review. Working backwards gives this chain:
- app fee paid;
- page with capsules and ≥5 real screenshots;
- ≥7 business days of review (US holidays excluded; late December runs slow);
- page public;
- registration.

A fest game therefore needs its concept and screenshots about 4 weeks before the registration deadline, ahead of its main development. A new partner's 15–30-day onboarding sits in front of all of it.
