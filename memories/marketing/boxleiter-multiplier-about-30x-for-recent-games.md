# Estimate competitors' Steam units as reviews x 30 (range 20-60), not the old 80x

- Area: marketing
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, desk research. Gamalytic 2023 (https://gamalytic.com/blog/a-deep-dive-into-the-steam-review-ratio); Game Developer, ~2018 (https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/using-steam-reviews-to-estimate-sales); Steam Page Analyzer 2026, unverified (https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/steam-review-to-sales-multiplier)
- Confidence: medium (±50% for any single game)
- Apply when: estimating comparable games' sales in market research (research/processed scripts).

The classic Boxleiter/Birkett figure of about 80x is outdated. The ratio halved after Steam began prompting players for reviews in 2019, and it still drifts down every year.

Adjustments:
- Very positive games (>90%): about 30x.
- Mixed games (about 70%): about 60x.
- Higher price: a lower multiplier.
- Small sellers (<1k units): about 20x (GameDiscoverCo).

Genre effects were not statistically significant in VG Insights' 2021 regression. Calibrate against our own units÷reviews after every launch.
