# Short-form video (TikTok, Shorts, Reels) is our primary free channel and doubles as the hook test

- Area: marketing
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, desk research. A Game About Digging A Hole: about 2–3 weeks of development, €4.99, more than 100k wishlists before its 2025-02-07 launch, 250k sold in week 1, viral on TikTok, Shorts and Reels (https://wnhub.io/news/finance/item-47099; https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/designed-in-just-a-few-weeks-this-usd5-indie-game-about-digging-a-hole-has-journeyed-to-the-centre-of-steams-top-sellers-chart); The Matriarch, about 400 to 20k wishlists from one TikTok (https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/from-400-to-20-000-wishlists-with-1-video-how-tiktok-can-make-your-game-go-viral)
- Confidence: medium (case studies are survivorship-biased; most are from 2022–23)
- Apply when: choosing a concept (it must read in a 10-second muted clip), and planning weekly marketing work.

Plan:
- Post 3–5 clips a week from the moment the concept is locked.
- Cross-post every clip to all three platforms. TikToks fade within about 48 hours, while Shorts keep resurfacing.
- Tag each link with UTM parameters so wishlists can be attributed to a platform.

Early clip performance is the cheapest signal we have of whether a hook works. A concept whose clips flop is a reason to reshape it before investing further.
