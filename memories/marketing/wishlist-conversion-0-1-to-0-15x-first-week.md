# Plan week-1 sales at 0.10-0.15x launch wishlists, and year-1 sales at about 0.7x

- Area: marketing
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, desk research for playbooks/marketing.md. GameDiscoverCo 2025-10-17 (https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/the-state-of-steam-wishlist-conversions); Zukowski "wishlist inflation" ~2026-10 (https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27273/steam-wishlist-inflation-value-of-1-wishlist-cut-in-half-but-predictive-power-remains-unchanged)
- Confidence: medium (read from search summaries; primary pages not fetched)
- Apply when: forecasting revenue for a concept, setting a wishlist goal, or judging whether a launch under- or over-performed.

Benchmarks:
- Median first-week sales ÷ launch wishlists is 0.15x for games with more than 25k wishlists (Sep 2024–Sep 2025).
- It is 0.10x for games priced above $10.
- Results vary about 10x between titles.

Sales per wishlist for 2023–25 releases:
- 0.22 in month 1 (it was 0.45 for 2020–22 releases);
- 0.43 at six months;
- 0.70 at one year.

Wishlists still predict sales well (correlation about 0.72). Each wishlist is simply worth about half what it was. Wishlists gained at festivals convert worse than others.

The week-1 review score is the biggest swing factor: over-performers averaged 91% positive, under-performers 67%.
