# Check that the team enjoys a genre before building in it

- Area: process
- Date / source: 2026-11-27, projects/002-cozy-organizing-sim/POSTMORTEM.md — Knolling Co. was the
  data-backed #1 direction (Organizing 5.4× lift) but the user found it "not enjoyable at all"
  because of the genre itself.
- Confidence: high
- Apply when: choosing a direction or concept from market research.

Market lift is necessary, not sufficient. Before committing, ask the user (via dialog) whether
they enjoy playing that genre and which games in it they like. Filter the candidate
directions by that, then by market data. Known: organizing/tidying games are out for this team.
