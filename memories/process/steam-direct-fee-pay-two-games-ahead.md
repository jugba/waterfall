# Pay each game's $100 Steam Direct fee at least 30 days (better: two games) before its launch

- Area: process
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/appfee ; https://partner.steamgames.com/steamdirect ; onboarding https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/onboarding
- Confidence: medium (30-day wait is official; whether it applies to every app for an established partner is unverified)
- Apply when: starting a new game project or setting up a new Steamworks partner account.

Each app costs $100 (recouped after $1,000 adjusted gross revenue) and Valve states a 30-day wait
between paying the fee and being able to release. First-time partner paperwork (bank, tax, identity)
takes 15–30 days; new TINs can take up to 30 days to validate. Only Admin users can pay the fee.
Rule of thumb for a monthly cadence: when game N launches, pay the fee for game N+2.
