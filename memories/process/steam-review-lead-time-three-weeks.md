# Submit the Steam build for review at least 4 weeks before launch; reviews take up to 12 business days

- Area: process
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, desk research for playbooks/steam-launch-checklist.md; Valve "Updates to the Pre-Release Review Process" (2026-11-03) https://store.steampowered.com/news/group/4145017/view/696528162820984745 ; https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/steam-says-its-running-behind-when-reviewing-pre-release-games-because-of-how-many-re-reviews-it-has-to-do/
- Confidence: high (Valve statement; may improve later — re-check each launch)
- Apply when: scheduling any Steam launch, especially on a monthly cadence.

Valve says pre-release build reviews now take up to 12 business days (normal 3–5) because of a
submission surge and re-reviews of failed builds. Store page review must pass first (submit ≥ 7
business days before it should go live). New checklist gate: the build must have recent playtime
launched through Steam (press reports ≥ 5 minutes) before it can be submitted. A failed review sends
you back into the queue, so a clean first submission matters more than ever. The draft
`playbooks/monthly-cycle.md` (build submitted in week 3) is too late.
