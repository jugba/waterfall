# Typical small-scope hits: ≤2 GB, single-player, 3–9 h median playtime; co-op raises reach but lowers the chance of ≥80% reviews

- Area: market
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, projects/001-steam-market-research (iteration 002): research/processed/analysis.md, reports/market-report.md
- Confidence: medium (SteamSpy playtime is partial)
- Apply when: scoping a one-month or ten-week game and deciding on multiplayer.

Profile of the 250 core hits:
- Median install size is 2 GB, and 151 are ≤2 GB.
- Median SteamSpy playtime is 4.8 h (p25 3.0 h, p75 8.7 h).
- 97% have single-player.
- Median of 11 languages; 41 shipped in English only.
- Median of 10 screenshots and 2 trailers.

Among ≥1k-review games, the share scoring ≥80% is:
- 88% for builds ≤1 GB, but 52–54% above 10 GB;
- 82% for single-player-only games, vs. 57% with co-op and 55% with multiplayer.

Co-op tags still have 3.4× core lift and the biggest upside: the median co-op core hit is about $771k (estimate). Co-op is a high-variance bet, not a default.
