# Price small games at about $7.99–14.99; the under-$5 tier is 42% of releases but has 1/6 the hit rate of $5–9.99

- Area: market
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, projects/001-steam-market-research (iteration 002): research/processed/analysis.md, reports/market-report.md
- Confidence: medium (the price gradient mixes in quality/investment selection)
- Apply when: setting a launch price.

Successes per 1,000 releases by list price:

| Band | Successes /1000 |
|---|---|
| $0.01–4.99 | 2.7 |
| $5–9.99 | 15.3 |
| $10–14.99 | 29.3 |
| $15–19.99 | 71.7 |

Core hits have a median price of $9.99, and 58% are under $10.

Median playtime by price, among ≥1k-review games:
- 4.4 h at $5–9.99;
- 7.0 h at $10–14.99;
- 5.8 h at $15–19.99.

Rule of thumb: about $7.99 for a 3–5 h game, and $12.99–14.99 only with 6 h or more.

A higher price does not cause success; it signals a more invested game. Do not raise price to look premium without the content. See also marketing/steam-refund-two-hours-short-games.
