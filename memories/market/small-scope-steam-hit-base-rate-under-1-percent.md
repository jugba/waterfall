# Under 1% of Steam releases become a small-scope hit (≥1k reviews, ≥80%); the median hit makes roughly $340k net

- Area: market
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, projects/001-steam-market-research (iteration 002): research/processed/analysis.md, reports/market-report.md
- Confidence: high for the counts; medium for revenue (Boxleiter estimate, ±50%)
- Apply when: forecasting a concept, setting expectations, or deciding portfolio strategy (one game per month).

Funnel for 30,708 releases in the 12 months to 2026-11-26:

| Stage | Share of releases |
|---|---|
| ≥10 reviews | 37% |
| ≥100 reviews | 11% |
| ≥1k reviews | 2.4% |
| ≥1k reviews and ≥80% | 1.7% |
| Core target (not major publisher, paid ≤$20, ≤10 GB, genuinely new) | 0.81%, i.e. 250 games |

The median release gets fewer than 10 reviews, and the 90th percentile gets 119, worth about $18k net at $9.99.

Estimated net revenue of core hits (reviews × 30 × price × 0.5):

| Percentile | Est. net revenue |
|---|---|
| p10 | $112k |
| p25 | $176k |
| median | $338k |
| p75 | $708k |
| p90 | $1.9M |

Even in the best tag clusters, only about 2% of releases become core hits. A monthly factory should plan on most games landing at 10–500 reviews, and on hits coming from many shots plus a strong hook test (short-form video).
