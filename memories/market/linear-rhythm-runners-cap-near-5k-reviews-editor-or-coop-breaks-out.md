# Linear rhythm runners/platformers top out at ~2–8k lifetime Steam reviews; breakouts add a level editor, co-op, or movement+charm

- Area: market
- Date / source: 2026-11-27, projects/004-rhythm-platformer/research/market-check.md §2; Steam appdetails `recommendations.total` + review histogram fetched 2026-11-27 (raw in projects/004-rhythm-platformer/research/raw/comparables/)
- Confidence: medium (survivor list from memory; launch prices partly unverified)
- Apply when: forecasting a music/rhythm action game or setting its upside case.

Lifetime all-language reviews for linear games with no editor and no co-op:

| Game | Reviews |
|---|---|
| BIT.TRIP RUNNER | 1.8k |
| Runner2 | 1.6k |
| Thumper | 4.1k |
| Melody's Escape | 4.3k |
| Melatonin | 7.6k |
| Sayonara Wild Hearts | 8.1k |

Sequels shrank: Runner3 has 263 reviews at 75%, and Melody's Escape 2 had 317 in its first 5 months. Art alone fails: Spinch has 140 reviews at 70%.

What broke out:
- UGC or a level editor: Geometry Dash 619k, A Dance of Fire and Ice 80k, Rhythm Doctor 26k.
- Co-op: Just Shapes & Beats 24k.
- Movement plus cartoon charm with no strict rhythm: Pizza Tower 67k.

Plan the base case at 2–8k lifetime reviews. Design level data so an editor or Workshop can ship later as the upside lever.
