# Organizing hits sell "chaos → order" at a counted scale, with unlocks that speed the player up

- Area: design
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, projects/002-cozy-organizing-sim/concepts.md §1 (data: projects/001-steam-market-research/research/processed/games_analyzed.csv, raw/appdetails)
- Confidence: medium (pattern across ~15 hits; review text not read)
- Apply when: designing or pitching any organizing, cleaning or sorting game.

Shared by Librarian, Supermarket Chaos, Sort Them Ducks, DinoBones, Cellar Keeper, Brick by Brick,
Bunker Tidy Up (1–30k reviews, 89–97%):
- The store pitch counts the mess: "3,072 books", "4,668 products", "18,490 bricks", "~2,000 bones".
- The visible payoff is the room or shelf filling up; Librarian even changes the music as shelves fill.
- Abilities unlocked by progress make the player faster, which gives the incremental feel
  (Relaxing + Incremental lifts 5.7×). DinoBones skips upgrades and still scores 95%, so "you get
  better at recognising things" can replace them.
- A collectathon log (items start "Unknown") and a one-line mystery or a cat add pull.
- Median play 3.6–11 h at $4.99–7.99.
Put the count in the short description and the fill-up moment in the first clip.
