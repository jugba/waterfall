- Area: design
- Date / source: 2026-11-27, user dialog after two no-go prototypes (prompts/2026-11-26_001_factory-setup.md, prompt 17)

- Apply when: choosing genres, hooks or what to polish first.

levels; art and charm come after the fun gate. Make an original game in this genre, not a copy of Rayman.
