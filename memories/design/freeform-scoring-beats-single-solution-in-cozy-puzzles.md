# In cozy organizing/craft games, freeform scoring rates far better than "one correct placement"

- Area: design
- Date / source: 2026-11-26, projects/002-cozy-organizing-sim/concepts.md §1.2 pattern 4 (scores from projects/001-steam-market-research/research/processed/candidates.csv, games_analyzed.csv; store text from raw/appdetails)
- Confidence: low (inferred from scores and store text; review text could not be fetched)
- Apply when: designing how a cozy puzzle, organizing or crafting game judges the player's result.

2D organizing puzzles that appear to have a single correct arrangement score 72–79%: Neat! ($9.99, 74%),
Suitcase Stories (72%), A Storied Life: Tabitha (79%). Games that promise freedom score 94–99%:
Dressmaker ("complete creative freedom", 99%), Cat Mail Co. ("no such thing as a correct way", 94%),
Scriptorium (97%). Prefer a scorer that rewards any tidy or beautiful result (alignment, grouping,
symmetry) and shows the reason for every point; keep strict solutions for optional bonus goals.
Verify by reading the negative reviews of Neat! and Suitcase Stories from a normal browser.
