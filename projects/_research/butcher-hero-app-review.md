# Review: Butcher Hero: Monster Idle RPG (iOS) — checked 2026-11-27

Source: App Store lookup API + recent-reviews RSS for id6757722046 (fetched 2026-11-27).

- Developer: MULTICAST GAMES LIMITED. Released 2026-01-16; v2.1.3 (2026-11-12). Free + IAP, 12+, 195 MB.
- Rating 4.69 from 11,668 ratings, but the 50 most recent reviews skew negative: 28×1★, 7×2★, 4×3★, 4×4★, 7×5★.
- Core loop: throw a hook at an enemy, pull it in, eat it → a permanent stat boost; absorb enemy traits
  (armor/speed/damage) for builds; evolve into a giant/razor form; items evolve.
- Praise: "just like the ad", "playable ad was accurate", fun, satisfying growth.
- Complaints (dominant): enemy/tree respawn timers rising to 5–24 h after ~30 min of play; pay-walled
  islands ("$800 to unlock all"); misleading IAP button placement; online required; bugs/data loss.

## Takeaway for the factory
The fun is the 30-second ad loop (hook → pull → eat → grow); the business model (timers + paywalls)
is what players hate. A premium Steam game can keep the loop and drop the timers. Any game we make
must be an original take on the mechanic (no Butcher Hero names, art or levels).
