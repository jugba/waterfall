# Iteration 002 — Data collection and analysis
- Date: 2026-11-26
- Prompts: prompts/2026-11-26_001_factory-setup.md (prompt 3 + dialog answers)

## Goal
Collect every Steam game released 2025-11-26 → 2026-11-26, enrich the ones with at least
1,000 reviews, and produce the market report.

## What we did
- Network: the Default environment blocked Steam. Created personal environment
  `waterfall` allowing store.steampowered.com, api.steampowered.com, steamspy.com.
- `research/scripts/collect_candidates.py` — walks Steam search (games, newest first),
  100 rows/page, 1 req/s; parses appid, date, review summary, price, tags, platforms.
- `research/scripts/enrich.py` — appdetails + SteamSpy for games with ≥1,000 reviews.
- `research/scripts/analyze.py` — funnel, genres/tags, price bands, months, medians,
  top small-scope successes.
- Found: `appreviews` API redirects to the store homepage, so review counts come from
  the search row tooltip instead.

- `research/scripts/fetch_extra.py` (new) fetched two raw datasets, cached in `raw/`:
  - Steam's tag-id → name list, saved to `raw/tags/`;
  - a review histogram for each of the 745 enriched games, saved to `raw/histogram/`.
- `research/scripts/analyze.py` was rewritten. It now covers:
  - data checks and corrections;
  - the "core target" segment;
  - tag hit rates against all 30,708 releases (opportunity/saturation), plus tag pairs;
  - price bands, playtime vs price, EA vs full release, weekday/month, size/language/player-mode scope signals;
  - revenue estimates and direction clusters.
- It writes `processed/analysis.md`, `processed/games_analyzed.csv`, `processed/tag_opportunity.csv` and `processed/summary.json`.
- Wrote `reports/market-report.md` and 8 memories in `memories/market/`.

## Results
- **Funnel:**
  - 30,708 releases;
  - 11,436 with ≥10 reviews;
  - 3,405 with ≥100;
  - 744 with ≥1k (one free prologue removed);
  - **530 successes**;
  - **332 small-scope successes**;
  - **250 core-target hits**: small scope, paid, first review no more than 45 days before release.
  - Source: `research/processed/analysis.md` §Funnel.
- **Data corrections** (`analysis.md` §Data checks):
  - 162 of 744 (22%) games with ≥1k reviews are EA graduates or re-releases whose reviews predate the window.
  - The search review count is language-filtered (all-language totals are a median 1.39× larger).
  - SteamSpy has real data for only 176 games, so tags now come from search tag ids.
  - Seven major publishers were missed (Bungie, HoYoverse, NCSOFT…) and are now re-flagged.
  - One free prologue was removed.
  - 57 joke prices (>$100) are excluded from price statistics.
  - No duplicate appids and no out-of-window dates.
- **Core hits:**
  - median $9.99, 2 GB, about 4.8 h playtime, 94% positive;
  - estimated median net revenue about $338k (p25 $176k, p75 $708k, p90 $1.9M).
- **Tags:**
  - Opportunity tags: Organizing 5.4×, Mining 4.9×, Investigation 4.5×, Job Simulator 4.0×, Co-op 3.4×, Relaxing 2.6×, Incremental 2.5×.
  - Saturated tags: 2D Platformer 0.19×, Turn-Based Tactics 0.23×, Arcade 0.39×, Bullet Hell 0.49×.
- **Candidate directions** (report §Candidate game directions):
  1. cozy organizing / job sim;
  2. juicy incremental with a gambling or roguelite hook;
  3. online co-op comedy (high variance);
  4. detective / investigation puzzle;
  5. nostalgia shopkeeper sim.

## What we learned
- Steam search tooltips count reviews in one language only. Use the review histogram or appdetails for totals. → `market/steam-search-review-count-is-english-only`
- Lists of "new releases" are polluted by EA graduates; filter by first-review date. → `market/early-access-graduates-inflate-new-release-lists`
- SteamSpy is mostly empty for 2026 games. → `market/steamspy-has-no-data-for-most-new-games`
- The base rate is under 1% even for the best clusters, about 2%. → `market/small-scope-steam-hit-base-rate-under-1-percent`
- Tag, price, scope and weekday findings were promoted to `market/` memories. See INDEX.md.
- Process: the histogram fetch took about 40 minutes at one request every 2–5 s. Fetch extra raw data early and in parallel with the analysis.
